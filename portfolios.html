What to include, at minimum

  • A short "about": your background, target role, and what career transition you're making
  • 2 to 3 writeups: a lab pentest, a home lab detection, or a mock audit, with the problem, your approach, and the outcome
  • Certifications and the study path behind them, not just a badge wall
  • A skills list scoped to your target track (SOC, offensive, GRC), not everything you've touched
  • A working way to contact you: email or a scheduling link
