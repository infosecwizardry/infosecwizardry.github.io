Recruiter-First Portfolio Flow Options

Primary audience: hiring managers and recruiters for defensive leadership roles. InfosecWizard stays visible as proof of learner impact, teaching ability, and practical cybersecurity communication.

Recommended

A. Defensive Leader Snapshot

Open with the exact leadership story, three measurable proof points, then guide recruiters through experience, capability areas, selected projects, and InfosecWizard evidence.

Mike Small
  • Fastest for recruiters because it answers “what level, what lane, what evidence?” immediately.
  • Lets InfosecWizard support the professional story without taking over the homepage.
  • Requires sharper copy and fewer top-level sections.
Best fit for defensive lead roles.
Option B

B. Capability Map

Make the homepage revolve around four clickable capability pillars: SOC leadership, detection engineering, purple teaming, and incident response.

Mike Small
  • Strong for hiring teams already looking for a specific defensive capability.
  • Makes your breadth understandable and interactive.
  • Can feel more like an index than a narrative if the hero is not very decisive.
Best fit when depth-by-skill matters most.
Option C

C. Operator + Educator

Lead with the dual identity: cyber defense leader in real environments plus InfosecWizard creator helping learners build practical capability.

Mike Small
  • Most memorable personal brand angle.
  • Great if you want hiring managers to see communication and mentorship as core strengths.
  • Riskier for recruiter scanning because the creator story may compete with the leadership story.
Best fit if the channel should be central.
