# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three audiences, often the same person at different times:

1. People trying to break into cybersecurity or pick up real skills (labs, CTFs, first detections).
2. People who want a crew: a community around cyber, not a course landing page.
3. Working practitioners hunting a higher-level role (senior, lead, specialist).

They arrive from a phone or a laptop, often late, often tired of generic "learn cyber in 30 days" funnels. They need to know in one fold who this room is for, and that the map and the resources live in one place.

## Product Purpose

**InfoSec Wizard** is the community. The site is the centralized roadmap and resource room for breaking into cybersecurity, and for the next role after that. The homepage is the front door. Later: Roadmap, Resources, Blogs, Labs, CTFs. Success is a stranger seeing the path, knowing the three lanes, and coming back when the map ships.

Wordmark is **InfoSec Wizard**, not "InfoSec Wizard Community". Community is what it is; it is not the name.

## Brand Personality

Direct, mechanical, unsentimental. Three words: **blunt, craft, crew**.

Copy sounds like a briefing, not a bootcamp ad. No fake metrics. No neon SOC cliché.

## Anti-references

- Navy-and-cyan "cyber" templates, circuit-board heroes, lock icons
- Udemy / TryHackMe marketing candy, countdown timers, fake student counts
- Inter-on-cream SaaS cards with identical icon+title+text grids
- Rainbow CTA rims, frosted pill nav, and the pink/yellow/violet/indigo swatch set
- Cloning another company's marketing chrome. Layout rhythm is allowed; their identity is not.

## Design Principles

1. **Say who it's for before you say what you have.** The three lanes are the homepage, not a footer note.
2. **Operators are proof.** Two working professionals, named as such, not a faceless org.
3. **Paper and ink, color as a rim.** Spectrum language: cool paper, spec-black bands, rainbow only on the CTA edge.
4. **Don't ship a 404 as a promise.** Unbuilt routes are marked soon, not linked dead.
5. **Craft over curriculum-speak.** Headlines in Impact. Body in a geometric grotesque. No em dashes, no hype.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor. Keyboard nav, visible focus, skip link, `prefers-reduced-motion`, 16px+ body, 44px targets, no `user-scalable=no`. Alt text is voice, not "image".
